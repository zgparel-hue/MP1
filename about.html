Tungkol sa Tindahan

Aling Nena has been running this corner store for years. Alam na niya ang usual order ng halos lahat dito, at kung sino ang madalas magpalista sa notebook.

Lately, her daughter has been helping out at the counter. Because of that, one regular suddenly started buying suka every single day, and somehow always forgets his sukli before walking away. There's also an old suitcase packed near the back, though Nena says Canada is still a long way off.

Same corner, same stories, same tindahan.
